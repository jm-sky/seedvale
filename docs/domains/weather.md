# Weather & Seasons Domain Roadmap

**Status:** evolving target vision

## Purpose

Time, seasons and weather should be world forces that influence terrain, resources, agriculture, fauna, NPC activity and presentation.

## Target state

- Seasons and weather evolve deterministically from world time and seed where appropriate.
- Weather changes environmental conditions rather than existing only as visual effects.
- Rain, temperature and seasonal conditions influence agriculture and natural resources.
- NPC and animal activity can respond to relevant environmental pressure.
- Water, terrain and vegetation can react to weather through shared world systems.
- Weather events create persistent or observable consequences where meaningful.
- Presentation reflects the same simulated conditions used by gameplay systems.

## Future evolution

1. Strengthen gameplay effects of existing weather.
2. Expand seasonal ecological and agricultural effects.
3. Add richer environmental responses and event chains.
4. Improve long-distance/off-screen continuity.

## Boundaries

Avoid duplicating weather state between rendering and simulation. Visual effects should derive from authoritative world conditions.

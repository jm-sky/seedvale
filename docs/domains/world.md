# World Domain Roadmap

**Status:** evolving target vision

## Purpose

The world should provide a coherent, persistent environment whose geography, time, resources and simulation continue independently of the player.

## Target state

- Terrain, water, climate, resources and places form one connected world model.
- World generation creates meaningful geography that influences settlements and ecosystems.
- Streaming and off-screen simulation preserve continuity without simulating everything at full fidelity.
- Time advances the same world processes regardless of player presence.
- Places and landmarks provide shared anchors for NPC routines, quests and exploration.
- Environmental changes can create persistent consequences and emergent stories.
- Rendering remains a representation of world state rather than the owner of simulation state.

## Future evolution

1. Complete current terrain, water and environmental systems.
2. Strengthen world queries and shared place semantics.
3. Improve hybrid/off-screen simulation continuity.
4. Add deeper environmental storytelling and world-scale interactions.

## Boundaries

Do not rewrite the world representation speculatively. Architectural changes must be justified by real product limitations.

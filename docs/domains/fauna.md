# Fauna Domain Roadmap

**Status:** evolving target vision

## Purpose

Fauna should form an ecosystem with needs, habitats, populations, reproduction, lifecycle and interactions with the wider world.

## Target state

- Animals have species-appropriate needs and behaviour.
- Habitat, food availability, weather and season influence activity and population.
- Predator/prey relationships create meaningful pressure on populations.
- Reproduction, mortality and migration or movement can change local populations.
- Domestic and wild animals use coherent lifecycle and simulation concepts where practical.
- Animal activity can affect resources, settlements and NPC decisions.
- Remote fauna can be simulated at lower fidelity while preserving meaningful continuity.

## Future evolution

1. Complete lifecycle and population foundations.
2. Deepen habitat and food-web relationships.
3. Add reproduction and population balancing.
4. Add migration and stronger settlement/ecosystem interactions.

## Boundaries

Species differences should primarily be data and behaviour configuration, not duplicated agent architectures.

# Agriculture Domain Roadmap

**Status:** evolving target vision

## Purpose

Agriculture should become a living production system connecting land, crops, weather, seasons, labour, food needs and settlement economy.

## Target state

- Fields have meaningful condition and maintenance state.
- Crops progress through explicit growth stages and respond to environmental conditions.
- Hydration decreases over time; rain and deliberate watering restore it.
- Weeds and other field problems emerge from world conditions and require labour.
- Crop failure and harvest create persistent consequences for households and settlements.
- Player and NPCs use the same field, crop and resource concepts.
- Seeds, harvests, storage, consumption and trade connect agriculture to the wider economy.
- Seasons and weather materially affect agricultural decisions.

## Future evolution

1. Core field maintenance and crop lifecycle.
2. Soil quality, fertility and stronger environmental effects.
3. Broader crops and production chains.
4. NPC farming routines and household/settlement logistics.
5. Storage, processing, food supply and trade integration.

## Boundaries

Agriculture should extend existing resource, item, NPC, settlement, weather and economy mechanisms rather than creating a separate simulation stack.

## Roadmap gaps

Detailed future mechanics should be added here as the target system becomes clearer.

# MPFB2 — Character Face Research

**Date:** 2026-08-28
**MPFB2:** 2.0.17
**Status:** `completed` ✅

## Purpose

Define a small set of reusable MPFB2 facial presets for Seedvale NPC generation.

The goal is not photorealism. Faces should have clear visual character and remain suitable for a stylized low-poly game.

---

## Female

Two female face variants were selected after visual comparison of generated combinations.

### HYB1

Selected as one of the best-looking female faces.

**Role:**
- general female NPC
- villagers
- workers
- neutral characters

### HYB2

Selected as one of the best-looking female faces.

**Role:**
- alternative female NPC
- visually distinct variation
- suitable for important/randomized female characters

> HYB1 and HYB2 are the current female base faces.

---

## Male

Two male face variants were selected.

### BLACKSMITH

Selected as the primary strong male archetype.

**Characteristics:**
- broad / heavy facial structure
- stronger cheekbones
- wider chin
- prominent chin
- wider and larger nose
- more rugged appearance

**Role:**
- blacksmith
- farmer
- labourer
- guards
- other physically demanding professions

### BARD

Selected as the primary lean male archetype.

**Characteristics:**
- narrower / more oval head
- slimmer face
- less facial volume
- more pronounced cheekbones
- narrower chin
- relatively expressive eyes
- more distinctive nose profile

**Role:**
- bard
- merchant
- scholar
- traveller
- other slim / agile characters

> BLACKSMITH and BARD are the current male base faces.

# Crime & Social Consequences\n\n## Vision\n\nCrime should emerge from ordinary world actions and their consequences for NPCs and communities. It should not become a separate player-centric "wanted level" system.\n\nNPCs should react to harmful or inappropriate actions they actually observe or learn about, with consequences expressed through existing social systems.\n\n## First version — keep it minimal\n\nThe first implementation will probably cover only a small, immediate reaction loop:\n\n    player commits a harmful action\n            ↓\n    NPC witnesses it\n            ↓\n    NPC reacts immediately\n            ├─ reaction sound\n            ├─ reaction animation\n            ├─ sympathy decreases\n            └─ reputation / standing decreases\n\nThe initial version should avoid adding memory, rumours, investigations, guards, wanted levels or other larger crime infrastructure unless required by the concrete implementation.\n\nThe goal is to make crime feel socially consequential with the smallest useful extension of existing NPC reaction and relationship systems.\n\n## Possible crime types\n\nPotential future categories include:\n\n- theft\n- assault\n- killing an NPC\n- killing a domestic or owned animal\n- damaging property\n- trespassing\n\nThese are ideas, not commitments for the first implementation.\n\n## Witnesses\n\nA witness should ideally be an NPC who could actually perceive the event rather than every NPC receiving global knowledge.\n\nPossible factors for later versions:\n\n- visual observation\n- hearing\n- distance\n- visibility / obstruction\n- NPC personality and traits\n- relationship with the player\n- relationship with the victim\n\n## Immediate reactions\n\nPossible reactions include:\n\n- protest / shout\n- surprise or alarm\n- flee\n- confront the player\n- defend the victim\n- seek help\n- attack\n\nThe exact reaction should depend on the existing NPC decision/reaction systems rather than becoming a separate crime AI.\n\n## Knowledge and social spread\n\nFuture versions could distinguish between:\n\n- directly witnessed events\n- information learned from another NPC\n- discovered evidence\n- rumours\n\nKnowledge should remain local and flow through existing social relationships where possible.\n\n## Evidence and traces\n\nFuture crime-related consequences could also emerge from persistent world changes:\n\n- corpses\n- signs of combat\n- dropped items\n- damaged property\n- missing goods\n\nThis could allow an NPC to discover that something happened without having witnessed it.\n\n## Social consequences\n\nCrime may eventually affect existing systems such as:\n\n- NPC sympathy / relationships\n- player reputation / standing\n- dialogue\n- trading\n- quests\n- household relationships\n- settlement reactions\n\nExisting social and reputation mechanisms should be reused instead of introducing parallel reputation state.\n\n## Future possibilities\n\nIdeas for later exploration:\n\n- reporting a crime\n- guards / authority\n- investigation\n- suspicion\n- wanted status\n- punishment\n- persistent crime history\n- witnesses remembering events\n- rumours spreading through settlements\n\nThese are deliberately backlog ideas, not planned implementation scope.\n
